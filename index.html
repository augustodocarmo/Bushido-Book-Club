01 — Why we gather

Knowing is not enough.

Bushidō asks something more demanding of us: that what we understand should eventually become visible in the way we live.

Every week we take one teaching from Bushido: The Soul of Japan, read it carefully, reflect on what it asks of us, discuss it together and then carry that question back into our own lives.

“To Know and to Act are One and the Same.”

We are not trying to consume another book. We are giving one book enough time to challenge us.

06 — Your host

Meet Augusto.

Augusto do Carmo has spent more than twenty years studying Bushidō and practising Aikidō. He is a 2nd Dan Aikidō practitioner, author and researcher focused on human behaviour, principles, consciousness and self-governance.

This Club is not built around the claim that one person possesses Bushidō. It is a place to study it seriously, question it honestly and attempt to live what we discover.

07 — Who it is for

You do not need to be a martial artist.

You need curiosity, seriousness and a willingness to examine your own life.

The Bushido Book Club is open worldwide. Martial artists are welcome. Complete beginners are welcome. The purpose is not to perform expertise; it is to study, reflect, discuss and practise.

09 — Questions

Frequently asked.

Do I need to practise martial arts?

No. The Club is open to martial artists and complete beginners.

What do we study?

The Club is dedicated exclusively to the study of Bushidō. Our base text is Bushido: The Soul of Japan, and each week we focus on one teaching.

When do we meet?

Every Monday at 19:00 UK time, online.

What languages are used?

The Club is designed to welcome members in English and Portuguese.

What happens if I miss a meeting?

Members have access to the recording and the discussion attached to that week's gathering.

Is the first meeting really free?

Yes. Your first gathering is free so you can experience the Club before deciding whether to become a member.
